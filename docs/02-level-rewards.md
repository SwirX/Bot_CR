# 02 — Level rewards

Three cadences. One is not enough to hold attention over a game measured in
years.

| cadence | reward | why |
|---|---|---|
| **every level** | R, `50 + 5 x level` | the bar always moves; never a dead screen |
| **every 5th** | cosmetic | visible to *other* players, costs the economy nothing |
| **every 10th** | milestone unlock + job perk | permanent convenience that compounds |

**Total R across levels 1-100: 57,750.** That is roughly 1.4x the 40,000 R daily
sell cap, spread across months — deliberately not enough to trivialise the
economy.

## Reward formulas

```
levelR(level)      = 50 + 5 x level
milestoneR(level)  = 500 * (level / 10)          # only at multiples of 10
prestigeR(level)   = 5000 * prestigeStep(level)^2
```

## Job perks

Unlocked at **10 / 25 / 50 / 75** in each job independently.

| job | 10 | 25 | 50 | 75 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| lumberjack | +5% log yield | +10% log yield | +15% log yield | +20% log yield |
| miner | +5% ore drop | +10% ore drop | +15% ore drop | +20% ore drop |
| builder | place freely in your own claim | +5% sell value | +10% sell value | +15% sell value |
| hunter | +5% mob drops | +10% mob drops | +15% mob drops | +20% mob drops |
| farmer | auto-replant | +10% crop yield | +20% crop yield | +30% crop yield |
| blacksmith | 10% faster smelting | 20% faster smelting | 30% faster smelting | 40% faster smelting |
| crafter | +25% craft XP | +50% craft XP | +75% craft XP | +100% craft XP |
| businessman | +10% daily sell cap | +20% daily sell cap | +30% daily sell cap | +40% daily sell cap |

Crafter buys **XP** back rather than output. Its `0.1` XP weight makes it the
grindliest job by rate, so an output multiplier on top would be nearly
worthless — the perk has to address the actual problem.

Businessman's `+10% daily sell cap` compounds with its job perks in
[04-unlockables.md](04-unlockables.md) and is the reason the job exists at all.

Unlocked at **5 / 15 / 25 / 35 / 45 / 55 / 65 / 75 / 85 / 95**.

| level | cosmetic |

| 55 | join-message colour: rose |
| 65 | particle trail: storm |
| 75 | chat tag: ascendant |
| 85 | particle trail: void |

**Cosmetics are never sold.** They are the visible half of progression; charging
for them turns the reward track into a bill and makes a level feel like an
invoice. Everything in the shop is earned with in-game currency instead.

## Which track pays

The R ladder follows **total job XP** — the sum of all eight jobs — not any single
job and not the AFK counter.

| option | consequence |
|---|---|
| **total job XP (chosen)** | one ladder, 57,750 R to level 100, matches the faucet budget in [05-economy.md](05-economy.md) exactly |
| each job's own track | 8x the budget: 462,000 R, because the ladder is paid once per job |
| highest single job | rewards specialisation but punishes spreading across jobs |

`gameplay_xp` is the **AFK counter**, not a levelling route. AFK exists to keep
chunks loaded near a mob farm and to stop players being attacked while away; at
1 XP per 30s it would take 675 hours to reach level 10 on its own. It is reported
but never used as a level.

`mc_profiles.level` therefore derives from total job XP, and so does the
published reward ladder. The two can never disagree.

## Payment semantics

R is credited **at the moment the level is reached**, not on a sync timer. The
server is authoritative and the wallet is SQLite-backed, so the credit happens
in the same transaction as the XP award. Appwrite mirroring follows on the next
profile sync and is display-only.

If a player is offline when an AFK or quest level-up occurs, the credit lands on
their wallet and is present on next login.

## Idempotency requirement

A player must never be paid twice for the same level. The wallet ledger keys
every grant on `(player_uuid, source, level)`, so replaying a grant is a no-op.
This matters because the profile sync, the AFK award path and the quit-persist
path can all observe the same level-up within one tick.
